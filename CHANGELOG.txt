[B]Changelog - Lootline v1.5.1[/B]

[B]Fixed[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Updated for the 2026-10-14 CS2 update. After it, storage units did nothing (CS2's storage window never heard that an item went in or came out), Uplink Terminal offers didn't show up on the laptop, and opening a case fell back to a slower way of showing the result. Lootline now reads that part of the game by itself, so an update that only moves the code around won't break it again.
[/LIST]

[B]Changed[/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]The Armory has its own tab[/B] - Cases -> Armory (the switch on top of the Cases tab): Armory on / off, your stars, +5 / +40, and this season's rewards with what each one costs (gold when you have enough stars). It's no longer in Settings.
[*][B]Name tags in the Inventory tab[/B] - a named item shows its name on the card ("blazer", with the gun under it), like CS2's inventory, and the search finds items by their name tag.
[/LIST]

[LIST]
[*]Left-handed (cl_righthand 0): stickers put on with Lootline can show mirrored on your first-person gun.

[B]Changelog - Lootline v1.5[/B]

[B]New[/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]The Armory with Lootline stars[/B] - CS2's own Armory (Store -> Armory) shows your Lootline stars. Redeem a collection, a case, stickers or charms there and you get it with the real odds, in CS2's own reward window. Settings -> Armory: on / off, your stars, +5 / +40. Only on this PC - nothing is sent to Valve.
[*][B]Storage Units[/B] - Add items -> Tools. Name it in CS2 (right-click -> Name), then put Lootline items in and take them out like a real one, with CS2's own storage unit windows, its item count and date.
[*][B]StatTrak Swap Tool[/B] - swaps the StatTrak counts of two Lootline guns in CS2's own swap window.
[*][B]Charm Detachments[/B] - a Charm Detachment Pack gives you 3. Taking a charm off a Lootline gun uses one, like in CS2.
[*][B]Loadout shuffle[/B] - CS2's shuffle works with Lootline items: mark them in a shuffled slot and every spawn picks one of them (your real ones too).
[*][B]Loadouts[/B] - Inventory -> Loadouts: save what you have equipped under a name, put a saved one on in one click, or delete it.
[*][B]Custom odds[/B] - Cases -> Random -> Custom odds: your own chance for each grade of each case (drag the slider or type the %). Off = the real odds.
[*][B]Random float and pattern[/B] - Add items and Cases -> Choose: "Random float" (a float of the picked wear that the finish really has - a Doppler only goes 0 - 0.08) and "Random seed". Every copy gets its own.
[*][B]Menu colour[/B] - Settings -> Menu: pick the menu's colour (the selected tab, buttons, switches, sliders) or turn on RGB so the colours go round. "Back to gold" puts it back.
[*][B]Tools in Add items[/B] - Storage Unit, Name Tag, StatTrak Swap Tool, Charm Detachment Pack.
[/LIST]

[B]Changed[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Trade-up floats work like CS2's: each input's float counts by where it sits in its finish's float range, and the result lands in the same place in its own finish's range.
[*]A skin from a case always gets a float inside its finish's real range.
[*]The 3D preview has the rarity colour behind the item.
[*]Graffiti: the can shakes first, then the spray sound, and the graffiti paints in.
[/LIST]

[B]Fixed[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Souvenir Covert (red) skins couldn't go into a trade-up contract.
[*]Trade-ups of blue and lower skins said "nothing of the next quality" when the skin's collection has no case (the Dust Collection's Glock-18 Brass, the Baggage and Achroma collections...).
[*]The game got slower after opening 100-150+ cases: the item list was saved after every single opening. It's saved at most every 1.5 seconds now.
[*]A deleted item flashed by when you scrolled your inventory: item ids are never used twice now.
[/LIST]

[B]Known issues[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Left-handed (cl_righthand 0): stickers put on with Lootline can show mirrored on your first-person gun.
[*]Pets (the chicken) may not work yet.
[/LIST]

[B]Changelog - Lootline v1.4[/B]

[B]New[/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]Lineups[/B] - your Lootline agent, gloves, knife and gun skins (stickers and charm too) in the start-of-match and end-of-match lineups, and on the side pick screen when you join a server. Only on your screen.
[*][B]New items like real ones[/B] - an item you add arrives as NEW: the Inventory tab shows the count, and opening it shows CS2's own "new item" window with the item in its rarity colour. Clicking through clears the count - it never gets stuck (Lootline answers it itself, nothing is sent to Valve).
[*][B]Major highlight charms[/B] - every one is in: Austin 2025 (492 clips), Budapest 2025 (373) and Cologne 2026. Inspect one in CS2 and its highlight video plays. Add items shows one card per Major and the clips in a list on the right, with a search for the clip, the player ("flamez"), the team or the map, and each clip's match ("VIT vs MOUZ - Mirage"). Every clip has its own market price.
[*][B]Scoreboard pin[/B] - equip a Lootline pin, coin or medal and it's next to your name on the scoreboard (Settings -> In a match, on / off). Only you see it.
[*][B]Name tags on the gun[/B] - a named Lootline weapon carries CS2's name tag on the gun, in first person too.
[*][B]Kill feed[/B] - your knife kills show your Lootline knife.
[*][B]Graffiti charges[/B] - an unsealed Lootline graffiti has 50 charges like CS2's ("Charges Remaining" in the inventory, "Charges" in Lootline's): every spray uses one, the last one uses it up. A spray makes CS2's spray sound now.
[*][B]New look[/B] - the rarity backdrop behind items is now like CS2's loadout card: the rarity colour strong on the left, fading into dark, with a fine mesh.
[/LIST]

[B]Fixed[/B]
[LIST]
[*]A weapon you picked up from someone else showed your Lootline skin (and hid its own).
[*]Graffiti only you see didn't show on official servers (it faded out at once when the map had been running for more than 6 minutes).
[*]A real item's own stickers and charm showed through your Lootline skin.
[*]The hotkeys (reapply skins, bunnyhop, FOV) went off while you typed in the chat or the console - an "n" in a chat message reapplied your skins. They're ignored while you type now, and bunnyhop doesn't jump on the space bar you type.
[*]A Lootline gun equipped in a slot where your Steam loadout has a different gun (MP7 / MP5-SD, M4A4 / M4A1-S...) - the buy menu sold the wrong one.
[*]Updated for CS2 build 14190: after that update every gun showed "Finishes: 0" and knives / gloves were missing in Add items (the game moved its list of finishes - Lootline now finds it by itself).
[/LIST]

[B]Known issues[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Left-handed (cl_righthand 0): stickers put on with Lootline can show mirrored on your first-person gun.
[*]Pets (the chicken) may not work yet.
[/LIST]

[B]Changelog - Lootline v1.3[/B]

[B]New[/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]Graffiti only you see[/B] - equip a Lootline graffiti and spray it with CS2's own spray wheel: it goes on the wall as a real graffiti decal (behind your hands and gun, lit like the wall, the game's own size), on your screen only. Nothing is sent to the server. One every 45 seconds like in CS2 - the wheel shows the cooldown.
[*][B]Sticker Slab[/B] - seal a sticker in a slab, crack it open again, hang the slab on a gun.
[*][B]Agent voice lines[/B] - your Lootline agent talks with its own voice, radio commands too.
[*][B]Currency[/B] - Settings -> Prices: show every price in dollars, euros, rubles, pounds, hryvnia, zloty, tenge, lira, real, yuan or shekel. The terminal laptop's "Accept Offer" shows it too. The rates are built in (Lootline never goes online).
[*][B]Inventory tab[/B] - click an item: a side panel with its 3D preview, stickers and charm, wear, pattern and its price (exactly that item: wear, StatTrak, Souvenir). Cards show their stickers and charm as small icons.
[*][B]One search for everything[/B] in Add items - skins, knives, gloves, agents, stickers, charms... ("ak redline" finds AK-47 | Redline), with a chip per category.
[*][B]Upgrader, reworked[/B] - your item, the wheel and the target big on top, your items and the targets below. Put up to 6 items in at once (their prices add up) and type the float the target comes with.
[*][B]Float typed everywhere[/B] - Cases -> Choose and Add items: type any float, or click FN / MW / FT / WW / BS.
[*][B]Medals and coins by grade[/B] - one card per medal / coin, its grades (bronze, silver, gold, diamond...) as pictures on the right.
[*][B]Graffiti colours[/B] - the white graffiti come in all 19 colours.
[*][B]Hotkeys[/B] for bunnyhop, FOV and "reapply skins", set right in their rows.
[*][B]Reapply skins when you spawn[/B] - everything (gloves too) is put on again a moment after you spawn.
[*][B]Locked loadout[/B] - in a match where CS2 locks the loadout, its loadout screen shows the Lootline items you forced in.
[*][B]X-Ray / loot box countries[/B] - Lootline cases open there like anywhere else; the X-Ray P250 Package is in the case list.
[*][B]Update prices[/B] - Settings -> Prices: one click opens CSFloat's price list in your browser, you save it, Lootline takes it. Built-in prices are from 2026-10-07.
[/LIST]

[B]Changed[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Lootline starts in the background: no freeze when it loads. Pictures in the menu load a few at a time, so opening a tab doesn't freeze the game either.
[*]A cleaner menu: bigger pictures that grow when there are few items, a rarity glow under the item, a centred "Remove Everything?" question.
[*]Prices: agents, charms (highlight charms too), Sticker Slabs, cases and keys now have their real market price. Stickers Valve never released (old Majors' gold / foil, the Katowice 2014 foils) are priced from the same sticker's real finishes instead of a cheap guess.
[*]The default T / CT agents are gone from the agent list; pictures CS2 doesn't ship are built in (the Redacted map coin, the Budapest 2025 charm).
